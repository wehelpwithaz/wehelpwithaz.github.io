Who we are

Walletz - Budget and Expenses (com.wehelpwithaz.walletz) is published by the independent developer WeHelpWithAZ, based in Morocco. Contact: learntique@gmail.com.

The principle: your data stays on your phone

Walletz requires no account and has no server that receives your information. What you enter is stored in the app's private storage on your device:

  • first name, currency, salary and payday you provide;
  • accounts (with their colours and logos), transactions, categories, fixed bills, debts, savings circles, budgets, goals, shopping lists;
  • photos of receipts or logos you choose to add;
  • loyalty points and preferences.

We cannot access it. It is neither sold nor rented. It leaves your phone only if you export it, or if you turn on the Google Drive backup described below.

Google Drive backup (optional)

This feature stays off until you connect your Google account (Profile, Backup).

  • What is sent: a copy of your Walletz data (accounts, transactions, settings, chosen logos and images).
  • Where: in the app data space on your Google Drive (“drive.appdata” permission). Walletz sees none of your other files or Drive content, and nobody but you can access it. We, the developer, cannot read this copy.
  • How: over HTTPS, directly between your phone and Google. Storage is then governed by Google's terms and privacy policy.
  • Your Google account: Walletz uses Google sign-in only to obtain this permission and to show the connected account address. It is not sent to us.
  • Control: you choose the frequency (after each change, daily, weekly, monthly), you can disconnect the account and delete the backup at any time (see “Retention and deletion”).

Features that use third-party services

Permissions requested

  • Notifications: reminders for fixed bills and salary (can be refused).
  • Internet: ads, Google Drive backup if enabled, promo code checks.
  • Advertising ID: added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.
  • Biometrics: for fingerprint lock, if you turn it on. Android does the recognition; Walletz only receives the result.

The camera, microphone and gallery are not used directly: Walletz opens your phone's camera, speech recognition or photo picker, only when you ask.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on the device as long as the app is installed. To delete everything: Android Settings, Apps, Walletz, Storage, Clear data, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling is final: export or back up first.

To delete the Google Drive backup: disconnect the account in Walletz, then in Google Drive open Settings, Manage apps, Walletz, and delete the hidden app data. For advertising data, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

Children

Walletz is intended for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children. We knowingly collect no data from children.

Your rights

Under Moroccan law 09-08 and, where applicable, the GDPR, you have rights of access, rectification, objection and erasure. Since we hold no data about you on our servers, these rights are exercised directly in the app (edit, delete, export, backup). For any question: learntique@gmail.com. You may also contact the CNDP (Morocco) or your country's data protection authority.

Security

Data is stored in the app's private space, protected by Android. You can add a code and fingerprint (Profile, Security). Network exchanges (ads, promo codes, Google Drive) use HTTPS.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with its effective date.
