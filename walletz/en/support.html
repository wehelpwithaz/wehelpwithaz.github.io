Help and frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the most common questions. Otherwise, write to us: we reply within 48 working hours.

Why does my budget start on payday and not on the 1st?

Walletz opens a new cycle when your salary arrives (or after the grace period you chose). A salary paid on the 28th therefore does not create a false deficit at month end.

What is “money left to live on”?

It is the real balance of your current and cash accounts, minus the fixed bills still due before the next payday. Divided by the days left, it gives your daily budget.

How do I add a transaction by voice or photo?

Tap the + button, then choose typing, dictation (example: “380 dirhams fuel yesterday by card”) or receipt scan. Check the filled fields, then save.

The scan did not find the right total

Photograph the receipt flat, well lit and in full. If the suggested amount is wrong, tap one of the other amounts shown under the message. You can send us the photo (hiding your personal details) to help improve reading.

How do I turn on Google Drive backup?

Open the Profile tab, then Backup, and connect your Google account. Tap “Back up now”, or leave automatic backup on: after every change, daily, weekly (recommended) or monthly.

Walletz can only reach a private, hidden folder in your Drive, not your other files. You can also save a backup file on your phone.

How do I get my data back on a new phone?

Install Walletz, open Profile, Backup, connect the same Google account and tap “Restore from Google Drive”. Your accounts, transactions, settings and logos come back. Without Drive, import your backup file in the same place.

How do I change an account's colour or logo?

On Home, in My accounts, tap Manage, then pick an account: colour, icon or an image of your choice (your bank's logo, for example). Logos are included in the backup.

How do I remove ads?

Profile, then Rewards: 150 points = 7 ad-free days, 260 points = 14 days. Points are earned by logging transactions every day and watching videos (3 a day). A promo code also gives ad-free days.

Can I export or import my transactions?

Yes: Profile, then Export and import. You can export to CSV and import a CSV file, an Excel file or a bank statement.

How do I protect the app?

Profile, then Security: turn on a code and fingerprint. Fingerprint recognition is done by Android; Walletz only receives the result.

How do I change the language or theme?

Profile, then Settings: income, light or dark theme and language. By default Walletz follows your phone's language.

How do I delete all my data?

On the phone: Android Settings, Apps, Walletz, Storage, Clear data (or uninstall the app). If you used Google Drive, delete the backup too: Google Drive, Settings, Manage apps, Walletz, then delete hidden app data. We hold no copy of your data.

How does the tontine work?

Each member pays the same amount every month and each takes the pot in turn. Walletz adds your contribution to your fixed bills and reminds you of the month you receive.
