Features

Everything you need to get through the month

Built for people who count every dirham, euro or dollar: clear, fast, no banking jargon.

A real amount left to live on

Your cycle starts when your salary arrives, not on the 1st. Walletz shows what is left after bills and your daily budget.

Entry in 3 seconds

Say “12 dollars coffee in cash” or snap the receipt: the total is read on your phone. Or just type.

Your accounts, your look

Bank, cash, savings: pick a colour and logo for each account and add photos to categories. Everything is recognisable at a glance.

Bills, debts and savings circles

Rent, instalments, subscriptions, tontine: reminders before the due date, one-tap confirmation and what is left to pay.

Budgets, goals, shopping

Set a limit per category, move toward your savings goals and prepare a shopping list with prices.

Google Drive backup

Optional: a copy of your data in the app's private space on your own Drive, so you keep everything when you change phone.

Getting started

Ready in three minutes

  1. Enter your salary and payday

    Walletz opens your cycle at the right time and prepares categories in your language.

  2. Add your accounts and fixed bills

    Bank, cash, rent, subscriptions: once, then everything is planned.

  3. Log expenses as you go

    By voice, photo or keyboard. What you have left updates by itself.

Your data stays with you

No Walletz account, no server: your transactions are stored on your phone. If you turn on Google Drive backup, the copy goes to the app's private space in your own account, which we cannot read. Walletz is free thanks to discreet ads that your loyalty points can remove.
