Privacy Policy

App: FocusHalo - Timer Pomodoro · Developer: WEHELP WITH AZ TECH · Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy explains what information the Android app "FocusHalo - Timer Pomodoro" (the "app") processes and why. In short: the app requires no account, and your tasks and statistics stay on your device unless you choose to back them up to your own Google Drive.

1. Data you create in the app

Your tasks, subtasks, settings (durations, language, options), your first name, your optional profile photo, your points and your focus session history are stored only on your device. We do not receive them, we do not transmit them, and we operate no server that stores them. You can erase everything at any time from Settings > Clear history, or by uninstalling the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob so that it can remain free. Depending on your consent choices, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and technical device information (model, OS version, language);
  • ad interaction data (impressions, clicks).

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other relevant regions, a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown on first launch. Ads are requested only after your choice. You can change it at any time from Settings > Privacy options, when that option is offered. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

No ads are shown during focus sessions. You can also hide banners for 3 hours by watching an optional rewarded video.

To learn more about how Google handles data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Permissions used

  • Notifications: to alert you when a session or break ends.
  • Exact alarms: so the notification arrives on time even when the app is closed.
  • Vibration: for the end-of-session signal.
  • Internet and network state: only to load ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by advertising, see section 2.

The app does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your data. The only data leaving your device is the data processed by Google AdMob as described above.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires) and does not knowingly collect data from them.

6. Security and retention

App data is stored in the app's private storage on your device, protected by Android. It is kept as long as the app is installed or until you erase it.

7. Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal data, there is nothing to export or delete on our side. For data processed by Google, you can exercise your rights (access, deletion, withdrawal of consent) with Google. You can also write to us with any question.

8. Changes

This policy may change. The last update date is shown at the top of this page, and significant changes will be mentioned in the release notes.
